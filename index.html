Prayer times, calculated on your phone.

Rowhni works out the five daily prayers from where you are, without sending your location anywhere. It also counts dhikr by listening, keeps your place in the Quran, finds mosques nearby and calculates zakat.

What it does

The five prayers, and the sun behind them

Times are computed from your coordinates using standard solar geometry, so they hold wherever you are. A sun path shows the whole day at a glance, and a Live Activity puts the countdown to the next prayer on your lock screen.

  • Several calculation conventions, so it can match your mosque
  • Adhan notifications you can set per prayer
  • Qibla direction from your position

A tasbih that listens

Say the dhikr and the counter follows. It recognises the phrases in Arabic and in transliteration, so you can keep your hands and your eyes off the phone. Tapping still works when speaking does not suit the room.

  • Recognition runs on the device
  • Targets for each dhikr, with the day's history kept

Your place in the Quran, kept

Progress is tracked by juz, surah and ayah, so picking up where you stopped does not depend on remembering. Translations and recitations sit alongside the text.

Mosques near you

A map of the mosques around your position, searchable by name, for the times you are somewhere unfamiliar and need to find jama'ah.

Zakat, worked out step by step

The nisab threshold in gold and in silver, updated with current metal prices, and separate entries for cash, gold, silver, business assets and livestock.

An encyclopedia to read through

The prophets and their stories, a hadith collection, and a quiz to test what stuck. Written to be read, not skimmed.

What leaves your phone

Short answer: your location does not. The longer answer is in the privacy policy, which is written to be read rather than agreed to.

Location
Used on the device to compute times and the qibla. Not transmitted, not stored on a server.
Voice
Dhikr recognition runs on the device. Audio is not uploaded.
This website
No cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests. Fonts are served from this domain.
Your account
You can delete it and its data without writing to anyone.

Try it for the next prayer

Free on both stores. If it does not suit how you pray, delete it; nothing is kept on our side either way.
