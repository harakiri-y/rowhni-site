Content sources

Qur'an text and translations

Arabic text, translations, tafsir and verse audio are retrieved from the Quran.com API.

quran.api-docs.io

Hadith

Sahih al-Bukhari and further collections, with their references and gradings, from Sunnah.com.

sunnah.com

Supplications

The supplication collection follows Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sa'id bin Ali bin Wahf al-Qahtani, with each entry's source given in the app.

Prayer time calculation

Prayer times follow the published parameters of the calculation methods offered in the app (Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, ISNA, Egyptian General Authority, Karachi, Tehran, Jafari and others).

Hijri calendar

Hijri dates use the Umm al-Qura calendar as implemented in the Java Time API.
