Comprehensive support for Rowhni. Troubleshooting, guides, and community help.

Getting Started

Essential setup guide for first-time users.

  • Initial Setup: Download from App Store (iOS 18.0+ required).
  • Permissions: Grant location for accurate prayer times.
  • Privacy: Location data stays on your device.
🕌

Prayer Times

Accuracy standards and troubleshooting.

  • Accuracy: ±1 minute with astronomical calculations.
  • Methods: Supports ISNA, MWL, Umm al-Qura, and more.
  • Fix: Check location settings if times are incorrect.
🎤

Voice Tasbih

Voice recognition tips and privacy.

  • Phrases: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar.
  • Privacy: 100% on-device processing. No recording.
  • Tip: Speak clearly in a quiet environment.
📖

Quran Tracker

Progress monitoring and reading features.

  • Tracking: Auto-tracks progress by Juz and Surah.
  • Sync: iCloud sync keeps progress across devices.
  • Audio: Download recitations for offline use.

FAQ

Is Rowhni free?

Core features are free. Rowhni Pro unlocks advanced analytics and removes ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes, prayer times, Qibla, and Voice Tasbih work completely offline.
